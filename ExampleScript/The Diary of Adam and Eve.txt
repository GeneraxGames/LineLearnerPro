
[Introduction]

VOICEOVER: In the beginning, there was nothing. The universe was a blank, sparse place, similar to the void between the prime minister’s ears. Then, for no reason, there was a loud bang. (comedy sfx) Having created the planets, God wanted to create life, but the question was, where would He put it? Mercury and Venus were too hot, Neptune was too cold, and Pluto was under contract to Walt Disney. Saturn and Jupiter were far too large, and for copyright reasons, Mars was barred. This left the choice between Earth and Uranus, and for obvious reasons, God chose Earth.

VOICEOVER: The Earth. Hopeless. Dead. Lifeless. A barren, dull and boring place, similar to Gosberton. Two parts water, one part land, ice and a slice of lemon, shaken and not stirred. Being somewhat of a novice at this stage, God thought He would experiment with simple, unintelligent life forms, and so He created “Actors”

[Scene 1]

(Stirring music, flashing lights while cast take their positions)

EVENAR: Day one. I arrived feeling like an experiment (Eve surveys audience). Around me is an incredible display of the most delightful objects. The flowers are beautiful and (Eve pets someone on the head in the front row) the animals are so ... friendly - not very bright ... but friendly. (Adam steps out of ready line, clutches his ribs and falls.)

EVE: (Eve moves to where Adam is lying) What are you?!

ADAM: Laying here in pain! Yourself? 

EVE: (Turns to audience) I haven't decided who I am yet. I’m thinking about it.

ADAM: Well, while you're thinking, could you help me up. I think I've had a thumper attack.

EVE: You mean a heart attack.

ADAM: (Eve helps him up) How do you know it's a heart?

EVE: I don't know. I just do. Being right all the time seems to be what I do best. (She points at his rib) Besides, it’s probably just your rib.

ADAM: (Shrugs off this idea) Oh. 

EVE: (staring at Adam) Say. You look just like me! 

ADAM: Nonsense! I am the only one of my kind. I know! I've checked everywhere. (Eve moves up to stand by him) Besides ... (viewing Eve up and down), you're not ... built right. I am supposed to be different from all the rest. (Moves forward to audience and stands hands on hip. Leans down to pick up rock) The maker made me from solid clod (Holds it in his hand); I was his final creation; and he has put me in charge of maintaining the park. Of course I am the only one! I am he! I am man! I am all!

EVE: (Comes down to him) Well to begin with (pointing to his hand), I'm fairly sure that THAT is a "rock" not a "clod," and you ... (looking Adam up and down)... and I don't want to hurt your feelings, but you don't look at all like you're made out of rock. Secondly, maybe your maker friend saved you for last because he just ran out of good ideas. And third - and I'm fairly confident here - that I was sent to give you all the help you could use. (long pause as she looks into his eyes) I think you need a lot.

ADAM: (to Eve) Oh really! Well, we'll see about that. (turning to audience) The Diary of Adam!

EVE: (sweetly) and Eve!

[Scene 2]

ADAMNAR: (Both readers begin on the stool with backs towards one another at a forty-five degree angle. Both will periodically write as they speak. The two mimes stand center stage. While this is going on, Adam and Eve look at one another and pantomime what the writers are saying.) Day two. (fiercely) Adam time! (Adam looks her up and down again, shakes his head and moves off. He will be followed by Eve) This new creature with the long hair, funny shape and the odd bumps is a good deal in the way. It is always hanging around and following me about. (Eve stands close and looks up at him sweetly) I wish it would stay with the other animals. (Pause) Maybe the alligators (He walks to other side of stage.)

EVENAR: (Eve turns to the audience) I followed the other experiment around yesterday afternoon, at a distance, to see what it might do. I was not able to make out what it is good for. It tapers like a carrot. I think it is a man. I had never seen a man, but it looked like one, and I feel sure that that is what it is. I was afraid of it at first, for I thought it was going to chase me, but by and by I found it was only trying to get away, so I tracked it along, several hours, which made it nervous and unhappy. At last it was a good deal worried, and climbed a tree. I waited a while, then gave up and went home. Today the same thing over. I got it up the tree again (Adam walks by, Eve bats her eyes. Adam backs off and slams into a tree. Eve turns to the audience.). Despite what it says, it seems an awful lot like me. Its hair is dark like mine. Our eyes are the same - only a different colour. Its skin seems the same. It walks upright as I do ... only .... it walks into things a lot. (Adam gets up and walks away.) I am not sure whether this is because it is larger and has not grown accustomed to its size or maybe it is balanced badly. (Adam hears this and turns to make a comment but trips over the rock). Perhaps it has poor vision and cannot find its way (Adam gets up, dusts himself off and walks to “tree staging area”).

ADAMNAR: I wish it would not talk. It is always talking.

EVENAR: (Eve goes to stand by the tree) I wish the new creature would not always go away and climb the tree. I would love to get to know it better. I would enjoy talking with it more. I love to talk. I could talk all day, and in my sleep too. I am very interesting to talk with. But if I had another to talk with, I know I could be twice as interesting and then I would never stop talking. I could talk and talk and talk and never run out of things to say (Adam screams and falls out of the tree. Eve looks down at him). Tomorrow I will get to know it better (Eve walks away. Adams moans and lays still).

[Scene 3]

ADAMNAR: Third day. The new creature and I had our first confrontation today. It did not go well. (Adam stands in front of imaginary pond with fishing rod, attempting to catch fish. Meanwhile Eve stands and watches him from the side. Finally, Adam catches one, yells in delight and does a celebratory dance)

ADAM: Hah! Yes! Got One! Woohoo!

EVE: Drop that fish you monster! (Eve reaches for the rock) Did you hear me? (Adam should react with fear)

ADAM: What do you think you're doing? Get away from here!

EVE: You throw that fish back!

ADAM: What fish?

EVE: (Points) That fish! 

ADAM: WHAT fish?!

EVE: (Points again) THAT fish! The one in your arms!

ADAM: This isn't a fish. (Smartly) It's a swimmer!

EVE: Fish!

ADAM: Swimmer

EVE: Fish

ADAM: Swimmer!

EVE: (brandishing rock) Oh yeah? Well what's this?!

ADAM: (insistent) A clod!

EVE: Well I'm going to clod you in the head if you don't drop that fish! (Adam sticks his tongue out. She throws. He ducks and barely holds on. She reaches to pick up another)

ADAM: Wait! Okay! Okay! (he tosses her the fish) Here!

EVE: (Eve grabs for the fish and drags it off while speaking) Don't you ever do that again, you bully! (Eve leaves. Adam stands in brooding fashion).  

ADAM: It used to be a LOT more fun around here.

[Scene 4]

ADAMNAR: Fourth day. Adam time. (Eve will come down across the front line of the audience naming things - dog, beaver, weasel, acorn, cabbage etc.) She fell in the pond yesterday when she was looking at herself in it, which she is always doing. She nearly drowned, which made her feel sorry for the creatures which live there, which she calls “fish”. She continues to fasten names onto things which don’t need them, and don’t come when they are called by them. Anyway, she got a lot of them out and brought them in last night, and put them in my bed to keep warm, but I have noticed them and I don’t see that they are any happier than they were before, only that they move less. When night comes I shall put them outdoors. I will not sleep with them again, for I find them clammy and unpleasant to lie among. The senseless naming by the new creature goes on and on. I've been examining the great waterfall. It is the finest thing in the park, I think. The new creature calls it -  

EVE: (Arms wide) Niagara Falls!

ADAMNAR: - why I am sure I do not know. 

EVE: It LOOKS like Niagara Falls! 

ADAMNAR: This is not a reason; it is mere waywardness and imbecility. I get no chance to name anything myself. The new creature names everything that comes along, before I can get in a protest. Always the same pretext - it LOOKS like the thing. Yesterday there was this flyer. She took one glance at it and said: 

EVE: It's a dodo. (Adam reacts) 

ADAMNAR: A dodo! No doubt it will have to keep that silly name. It's ridiculous!

EVE: Anyway, I haven’t even seen it fly, so how can it be a flyer?

ADAM: Because it has those big flappy arms, so obviously ...

EVE: You mean wings?

ADAM: Oh, good grief! It has big flappy ARMS, so OBVIOUSLY it’s a flyer!

EVE: It doesn’t fly, it has wings, and it’s called a DODO!

ADAM: A dodo? (shakes head) I give up. It looks no more like a "dodo" ... than I do.

EVENAR: Thursday. We are getting along better and better all the time now and better and better acquainted. He does not try to avoid me now, and that shows how much he likes me. This pleases me and I try to be useful to him in every way that I can. (Adam comes off of line to stand down stage. Eve bounds up to him. Adam takes one look at her and pushes her over by the forehead.)

ADAMNAR: The new creature continues to follow me around. I don't know why it does that. (Eve gets back up and wanders over to Adam.) Doesn't it have anything better to do? She is always touching me (Eve stands shoulder to shoulder). I am not used to this. She comes too close ... (by this time Adam should realize Eve's presence and turns to slowly face her. Both should look closely into one another's face, but Adam backs down first. Eve may chose to smile as she finds this innocent curiosity) .... to me. (Adam turns back to his book and begins to write) She also makes me very nervous. (Adam backs away nervously. Eve sighs, then goes to centre).

EVENAR: During the last two days I have taken all the work of naming things off of his hands, for he has no gift in this area and appears to be very grateful (Adam moves down threatening to strangle her). Also, he talks very little. Perhaps it is because he is not very bright and wishes to hide this. So, whenever a new creature comes along I name it before he has a chance to expose himself and be embarrassed. In this way I have saved him many times and he seems enormously grateful. 

[Scene 5]

ADAMNAR: (Glumly) Fifth Day. Adam time. She has taken up with a snake now. The other animals are glad, for she was always experimenting with them and bothering them; and I am glad because the snake talks, and this enables me to get a rest. In addition, she engages herself in many foolish things: among others - trying to figure out why lions and tigers live on grass and flowers when their teeth indicate that they should eat each other. This is foolishness!

ADAM: To do that they would have to “kill” each other and that would introduce - as I understand it - something called "death." From what I have been told this has not yet entered the park (a glance at Eve). Too bad!

ADAMNAR: (Adam goes up the tree) My life is not as happy as it was. The new creature eats too much fruit. Most likely, we are going to run short. I had to clod it out of the apple tree the other day. (Eve comes down centre) It has no idea how much trouble we could be in. "We." That is its word. Mine too now from hearing it so much. The new creature says its name is ...

EVE: Eve (Smiles sweetly)

ADAM: (Talking to audience) It says I should use it whenever I want it to come. (Lays back) Like that will happen anytime soon. (Adam leaves tree to sit in the classic “Thinker” position). Oh yes. It says that it is not an "it" but a "she." It is all the same to me. I just wish SHE would leave ME out of it and not make such an issue of it. 

EVE: (bouncing up) Do you know what my name is?

ADAM: (drops head) Oh cripes!

EVE: No. Not cripes! Eve. (sits down) My name is Eve. And you may call me that whenever you want to beckon me.

ADAM: That would be superfluous!

EVE: (to audience) Where did he get that word? I don't think I've ever used it.

ADAM: I'm going to make a shelter - for myself! ME! I! IT!

EVE: (to audience) It's strange that my name doesn't interest him. If he should tell me his, I would care. It would be pleasanter to my ears than any other sound. (To Adam) What is your name?

ADAM: (firmly) I do not wish to be disturbed.

EVE: Oh. (Pause. Turns to audience) That’s rather complicated. Do you have a nickname? 

ADAM: NO!  Just leave me alone!

EVE: (To audience, sweetly and with great patience) Once again, he is too shy to ask for my help. (Turning to Adam) Would you like me to help you remember it?

ADAM: You are not going to name ME for I have a name and I know what it is!

EVE: Well, you're definitely not an Albert or Archibald or an Alphonso ...

ADAM: Of course I'm not an Alphonso. My name is Adam!

EVE: (Pause while she looks him over) You know. You look like an Adam. 

ADAM: That's because I AM Adam!

EVE: That just goes to prove my point.

ADAM: What point?

EVE: That if something looks like something, well, THAT’S what it is.

ADAM: Well that's how much you know. What's (looking around and pointing over audience) ... that over there?

EVE: It's a parrot.

ADAM: Why do you call it a parrot?

EVE: (She steps up to point at it while Adam mimics her) Because it LOOKS like a parrot.

ADAM: Well not to me it doesn't. It looks like a ... like a .... like a loud-mouthed fat beak.

EVE: Nevertheless, it's a parrot.

ADAM: (exasperated) What makes you so sure?

EVE: It's just a talent I have.

ADAM: Oh really. (Adam points to various people in the audience and Eve fires back with ease) What's that?

EVE: A horse.

ADAM: That?

EVE: A lizard.

ADAM: That?

EVE: A goat.

ADAM: That?

EVE: A mushroom.

ADAM: That?

EVE: A gerbil.

ADAM: That?

EVE: A turnip.

ADAM: (angry) You're just GUESSING!

EVE: I'm not guessing. Those are their names because that's ... (Adam cuts her off)

ADAM: Don't say it! How old are you?

EVE: Four days.

ADAM: (Shaking fist) Well you’re NOT going to live to be five!

EVE: (moving closer to him) Oh dear. I've hurt your feelings.

ADAM: Don't be ridiculous.

EVE: Yes I have. I can tell. I'm so sorry.

ADAM: Well, don't be, because you're wrong. Anyway, I can't waste any time here (starts to leave) I have to go empty the four-pronged white-squirter.

EVE: (Long pause) Do you mean the “cow”?

ADAM: (Scream! And exit)

[Scene 6]

ADAMNAR: Day 6. The new creature seems to be trying to do everything she can to make it impossible to understand her. No sooner is it that I start to come to terms with her strange way of thinking, that she then goes and changes the rules. I have no doubt that even if we stay together for thousands of years, that I will be no closer to knowing how her mind works than on the first day

EVENAR: Adam is warming to me more and more now, which pleases me greatly. We still have some misunderstandings, but on the whole, I am sure he is beginning to like me very much, and I am sure that we will continue to be very happy together. We spent a lovely afternoon lying back on the grass, looking at the sky and chatting, and I even managed to make him laugh, which I am sure is a good thing for us. I am not sure what he found amusing, but even so, I feel that this is a great step forward in our relationship

ADAMNAR: She claims that we could be very happy together, but this seems ridiculous to me, as I was very happy before she came along, so why should her presence make me happier? All that has happened is that everything has become much more confusing

(Eve adopts reclined position to look up at the sky, Adam joins her as she starts speaking)

EVE: What a beautiful day! Adam! Come and sit down with me and look at the clouds

ADAM: The what?

EVE: The clouds. Aren’t they lovely and fluffy?

ADAM: Can I assume, therefore, that you have decided to call the white sky puffs “clouds”?

EVE: Oh Adam, I knew you would start to think like me in time. Yes, they are clouds, because ...

ADAM: Because they look like clouds?

EVE: That’s wonderful Adam, we are really starting to understand each other now

ADAM: Hmmmm

EVE: Oooh, look at that one (pointing out a cloud) – it looks like a sheep

ADAM: So it’s a sheep now?

EVE: No, silly, it’s a cloud, but it just looks like a sheep

ADAM: But you have said that if something looks like something, then that is what it is

EVE: Yes, that’s right, but only if it is not something else

ADAM: So does that mean that the wooly creatures in the park are now going to be called clouds?

EVE: Of course not, they’re sheep

ADAM: But that’s a cloud up there. That looks like a sheep. But isn’t a sheep. Even though it looks like something that it isn’t?

EVE: Oh, you are clever Adam, I knew you would work it all out

ADAM: So is that one a rabbit? (pointing out another cloud)

EVE: No, it’s a cloud that looks like a rabbit

ADAM: But ...

EVE: Isn’t this simply wonderful, Adam? This is the first time we have sat down together and enjoyed each others company.

ADAM: Enjoyed? ...

EVE: I know that if we have more and more time spent together like this, that our relationship will grow and grow, and we will be happier and happier together

ADAM: Relationship? ...

EVE: And you will want to spend more and more time with me, and we will both live happy lives together, with me helping you to understand the world and all the things in it, and you learning to understand me. Oh look, there’s a fish! (pointing out another cloud)

ADAM: I think you may be a little confused here. There is more chance of man flying to the night sun one day than us ever living a happy life together

EVE: You mean the moon?

ADAM: I mean the ...(gives up) Can I ask you a question?

EVE: Of course you can, Adam, you can ask me anything at all, and you know I will always give you the right answer, because that is what I am good at

ADAM: Do you think the clouds look down on us and think about what things here look like?

EVE: Oh, er, well, I am sure they must do, yes

ADAM: And what do you think the clouds say when they look at me?

EVE: They probably say “Look at that one – isn’t he handsome. He looks like he is very happy. I bet it is because he is really glad that Eve came along and fulfilled his life. If they spent all their time together, I think he would be even happier.” (Eve is hopeful that her advances are accepted – Adam, meanwhile, is aghast at the idea) What do you think they say when they look down and see me?

ADAM: You really want to know?

EVE: Of course

ADAM: They probably say “Look at that one. It looks like an idiot”

(Adam laughs and goes off stage)

[Scene 7]

EVENAR: He showed no interest in my name (sigh). I tried to hide my disappointment, but I suppose I did not succeed. So I went away and sat on the moss-bank and looked into the water. It is where I go when I hunger for companionship, someone to look at - someone to talk to. It is not enough - still it is better than nothing. It talks when I talk. It is sad when I am sad. It comforts me with sympathy. It is a good friend - my only one. Three days he has been gone. It is a long time to be alone. Still, it is better than being unwelcome. Mostly, these days have been good times - not lonesome at all! (Eve looks towards ADAMNAR) Well ... not too much. (Eve drops her head and looks sad).

(Adam comes on stage, dragging on shelter he has built)

EVE: Good morning! What are you doing? (Adam ignores her) Can I help? Are you hungry?

ADAM: Go away numbskull!

EVE: (Sighing despondently) My name happens to be Eve. In the future, kindly use my name when you wish to speak to me or when you wish to call me. That's what it's for. (Pause) Can I help?

ADAM: No (Adam sits in his shelter).

EVE: I think it's going to rain ....... May I come in? (Eve kneels by the entrance to the shelter and her head should almost be over Adam’s as he is lying on the ground facing the audience).

ADAM: It's crowded

EVE: I don't mind

ADAM: I do!

EVE: Why do you hate me so much?! I just don't understand it. I'm a very nice person!

ADAM: Good. Go play with the lions.

EVE: I'm interesting too. And if you'd only talk to me nicely, I'm sure I could be twice as interesting to you.

ADAM: You want to talk? Listen to this: GO AWAY! (Eve reacts with great hurt that slowly evolves into tears as she hides her face in her hands and leans forward over Adam. Adam continues to fume till he notices something coming from Eve above him. Slowly he holds out his hand - palm open - and then glances at what he sees in it.)

ADAM: Hey. Um ... (hesitantly) Eve ... you're leaking.

EVE: I am NOT leaking - I'm CRYING! But don't let it disturb you! I know YOU don't care! (Eve puts her face back in her hands)

ADAM: I don't ... really ... well ... a little ... it's just that I don't like to see you ... you know ... so ...(SIGHS) I suppose there might be ... some spare room in here.

EVE: (Cautiously) Really?

ADAM: Well I guess you could come in.

EVE: Are you sure? I don't take up much room and I promise I won't talk at all unless you want me to. (Eve moves into the shelter) Oh Adam. It will be like our very first home.

ADAM: (Rolls eyes) Why do you call it a "home?"

EVE: Because it LOOKS like a home. (Adam reacts). Don't worry Adam. It will be wonderful. Really. Don't be afraid. (pause as she looks at his shaking form) Here. Have an apple. (Adam sits up and stares big-eyed at the audience) 

EVE: The end

ADAM: How do you know it’s the end?

EVE: Because it looks like the ...

ADAM: Don’t SAY it!

EVE: ... (Sweetly) end
